Code on Social Security, 2020

A practical guide to EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefit, employees’ compensation and social-security schemes.

Content version: 1.0 · Reviewed: 19 November 2026 · General employer guide

← Four Codes overview
Legal-status and applicability notice

The Code consolidates central laws relating to social security, including provident fund, employees’ state insurance, gratuity, maternity benefit, employees’ compensation and provisions for specified worker groups. Its commencement notification was corrected; the corrected Gazette instrument must be read with the original notification. This page is educational guidance, not a legal opinion. Confirm the particular provision, commencement instrument, appropriate Government, rules, scheme and establishment facts before taking action.

Coverage and scheme-based compliance

  • Determine coverage separately for the establishment, employee category and relevant chapter or scheme. Do not assume one threshold or definition controls every benefit.
  • Confirm the appropriate Government, location, headcount, wage data, activity, exemptions and any voluntary coverage.
  • Read the Code with applicable schemes, rules, notifications and official directions. The Code alone does not answer every contribution, ceiling or filing question.

Employees’ Provident Fund and related schemes

  • Map establishment coverage, employee membership, exclusions, wage components and contribution liability under the Code and applicable EPF scheme framework.
  • Check the currently applicable EPF Scheme, Employees’ Pension Scheme and Employees’ Deposit-Linked Insurance Scheme, together with amendments, corrigenda and notifications.
  • Maintain onboarding and exit data, UAN records, nomination information, wage/contribution workings, ECR acknowledgements, challans and payment evidence.
  • Do not rely on an old contribution-rate or wage-ceiling chart. Verify the current scheme and official notification before payroll processing.

Employees’ State Insurance

  • Assess establishment and employee coverage under the Code and applicable ESI rules/scheme, including any notified extension or special category.
  • Verify current wage ceiling, contribution rates, contribution periods, benefits and reporting processes from the official ESIC/Ministry source.
  • Maintain employee registration, insurance particulars, wage records, contribution calculations, payment acknowledgements and accident-reporting records.

Gratuity

  • Assess eligibility, continuous service, calculation, nomination, payment timeline and statutory exceptions under the Code and applicable rules.
  • Do not apply a simple five-year test without checking the exact statutory provision and relevant exceptions, including death, disablement and fixed-term employment where applicable.
  • Maintain joining date, service history, wage basis, nomination, separation date, calculation sheet, approval and payment evidence.

Maternity benefit

  • Review eligibility, qualifying service, duration of benefit, nursing breaks, crèche-related duties where applicable and protection against prohibited dismissal or disadvantage.
  • Distinguish the maternity-benefit chapter from ESI-covered benefit arrangements and identify the statutory route that applies to the employee.
  • Keep applications, leave records, payment calculations and prescribed registers, handling sensitive medical information appropriately.

Employees’ compensation and employment injury

  • Identify when compensation liability arises, including employment injury and occupational disease, using the Code’s statutory tests.
  • Maintain accident reports, investigation records, medical evidence, wage/service details, compensation calculations and payment/settlement records.
  • Coordinate HR, safety, insurance and legal teams so reporting and benefit processes are not missed.

Unorganised, gig and platform workers

  • The Code contains registration and social-security scheme provisions for unorganised workers and specified gig/platform worker categories.
  • Do not equate an enabling registration or scheme provision with an automatically payable employer contribution in every case. Identify the relevant scheme, notification, statutory category and contribution basis.
  • Keep the actual scheme/notification and a written applicability assessment before making or withholding a contribution.

Corrected commencement and Central Rules

  • Read S.O. 5319(E) dated 21 November 2025 together with corrigendum S.O. 5936(E) dated 19 December 2025. The corrigendum changes entries in the commencement notification; do not rely on the initial PDF alone.
  • The Ministry repository lists the Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 as G.S.R. 344(E), dated 8 May 2026. Check the applicable scheme and later notifications as well.

Employer action plan

Use these as starting controls. Convert them into a live compliance checklist only after confirming legal applicability to the establishment.

01. Coverage assessment

Maintain separate coverage conclusions for EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity, employees’ compensation and other relevant provisions.

02. Payroll configuration

Version-control wage definitions, contribution bases, rates, ceilings and effective dates with source references.

03. Employee lifecycle

Control joining, UAN/insurance registration, nominations, transfers, exits and settlements.

04. Monthly reconciliations

Reconcile payroll, ECR/contribution files, challans, payment proofs and employee-wise ledgers.

05. Benefits administration

Use documented processes for gratuity, maternity benefit, employment injury and claims.

06. Scheme monitoring

Track Ministry, EPFO and ESIC notifications for changes to schemes, rates, ceilings, coverage and filing processes.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Social Security Code mean EPF and ESI are no longer administered through schemes?

No. The Code is a statutory framework; the applicable schemes, rules, notifications and official processes must still be read and applied.

Does gig/platform registration automatically create a contribution duty for every aggregator?

No. Verify the statutory category, scheme, notification and contribution conditions that apply.

Can payroll continue using old EPF/ESI rates and ceilings without checking?

No. Confirm current rates, ceilings, coverage rules and effective dates through EPFO, ESIC and Ministry instruments.

Is five years’ service always required for gratuity?

Do not apply the general rule without checking statutory exceptions and the provisions relevant to the particular employment situation.
