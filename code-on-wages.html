Code on Wages, 2019

A practical employer guide to minimum wages, floor wage, payment of wages, deductions, overtime, bonus and equal remuneration.

Content version: 1.0 · Reviewed: 19 November 2026 · General employer guide

← Four Codes overview
Legal-status and applicability notice

The Code consolidates the central framework relating to wages, minimum wages, payment of wages, bonus and equal remuneration. Read it with the applicable commencement notification, rules, wage notifications and exemptions. This page is educational guidance, not a legal opinion. Confirm the particular provision, commencement instrument, appropriate Government, rules, scheme and establishment facts before taking action.

Coverage and applicability

  • Identify the establishment, activity, employee categories, work locations and appropriate Government. Do not decide coverage only by the organisation’s name or industry label.
  • Check whether the particular obligation applies to all employees or depends on a statutory definition, wage ceiling, establishment threshold, scheduled employment, notification or exemption.
  • Preserve a written applicability note that records the facts, legal provision, applicable rule/notification and review date.

Definition of wages and remuneration mapping

  • Review each remuneration component against the definition of “wages” in section 2(y), including basic pay, dearness allowance, retaining allowance and the statutory exclusions.
  • The definition includes an add-back mechanism where specified excluded components exceed the stated proportion of remuneration. The calculation must follow the statutory definition; it is not a general instruction that every employee’s basic pay must equal 50% of CTC.
  • Prepare a component-wise mapping sheet. Do not automatically reuse a wage definition or calculation method from another statute without checking that statute’s own provisions.

Minimum wages and floor wage

  • Identify the current minimum-wage notification of the appropriate Government for the relevant employment, skill/category, geographical area and effective period.
  • Check the Code’s floor-wage framework and the relationship between the floor wage and minimum rates fixed by the appropriate Government.
  • Retain the exact notification, category mapping, rate table and effective date used for each payroll period. An old rate chart is not enough.

Wage period, payment and deductions

  • Configure the wage period and payment timeline by reference to the applicable statutory provision and rules.
  • Map each deduction to a category permitted by the Code and retain the supporting authorisation and evidence. Employee consent or an internal policy does not by itself make an otherwise unauthorised deduction lawful.
  • Review final wage settlement timing for resignation, dismissal, removal, retrenchment, closure and other cessation events under the applicable provisions.

Overtime and working-time interface

  • Review the overtime entitlement and statutory rate under the Code, including the not-less-than-double-rate rule where applicable.
  • Working hours, rest intervals, spread-over and weekly rest are also governed by the OSH Code and applicable rules. The wage calculation should be checked with the relevant working-time requirement.
  • Maintain attendance, shift, overtime approval and payment records.

Bonus

  • Assess eligibility, applicable wage ceiling, minimum service conditions, disqualification provisions and the relevant accounting year.
  • Check the statutory minimum/maximum bonus framework, allocable surplus and applicable calculation rules.
  • Do not reuse a historic wage ceiling or bonus worksheet without confirming current notifications and rules.

Equal remuneration and non-discrimination

  • Review recruitment, wages and conditions of employment for prohibited discrimination on the ground of gender in relation to the same work or work of a similar nature, subject to the statutory wording.
  • Maintain job descriptions, grading criteria, wage bands and records that explain objective differences in role, skill, responsibility or performance.

Registers, wage slips and inspection readiness

  • Confirm the registers, wage slips, notices, forms, language, electronic-maintenance and retention requirements in the rules made by the appropriate Government.
  • Keep wage calculations, attendance, overtime, deductions, bonus workings, minimum-wage mapping and payment evidence retrievable.
  • For Central-sphere establishments, consult the Code on Wages (Central) Rules, 2026. For State-sphere establishments, check the relevant State rules and notifications.

Employer action plan

Use these as starting controls. Convert them into a live compliance checklist only after confirming legal applicability to the establishment.

01. Wage structure review

Prepare a complete remuneration-component inventory and map each item to the Code’s definition of wages.

02. Minimum-wage matrix

Maintain location-, employment- and skill-wise rates with the official notification and effective date.

03. Payroll controls

Check wage period, payment cut-off, authorised deductions, overtime and exit settlement.

04. Bonus control

Record eligibility, applicable ceiling, calculation method and payment evidence.

05. Records and notices

Confirm forms, registers, wage slips, notices and retention requirements under the correct rules.

06. Legal-change control

Review notifications and amendments before the relevant payroll period.

Frequently asked questions

Does one minimum-wage rate apply across India?

No. The applicable rate depends on the appropriate Government’s notification, employment/category, skill classification, location and effective date.

Does the wage definition mean basic pay must always be 50% of CTC?

No. Apply the statutory definition and add-back mechanism to the actual remuneration components. CTC is not itself the statutory calculation base.

Can an employer deduct any amount if the employee signs consent?

No. A deduction must be authorised by the Code and meet the applicable conditions.

Can the old bonus ceiling be used automatically?

No. Confirm the currently applicable notification and rules before determining eligibility and calculation.
