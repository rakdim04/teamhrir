Implementation: do not confuse four separate questions

Commencement

21 November 2025 is the commencement date appointed in the notifications for the Codes, but the operative wording must be read Code by Code. The Wages notification lists specified provisions; the Industrial Relations and OSH notifications use different wording; and the Social Security notification was corrected by a later corrigendum.

Code Commencement instrument What to verify
Code on Wages, 2019 S.O. 5322(E), 21 Nov 2025 Notification lists the provisions appointed to commence; check the section-level list and exceptions.
Industrial Relations Code, 2020 S.O. 5320(E), 21 Nov 2025 Read with Industrial Relations Code (Amendment) Act, 2026, particularly its amendment to section 104.
Code on Social Security, 2020 S.O. 5319(E), with S.O. 5936(E) corrigendum dated 19 Dec 2025 Use the corrected notification, not the original notification in isolation.
OSH & Working Conditions Code, 2020 S.O. 5321(E), 21 Nov 2025 Check applicable Central/State rules and any chapter-specific notifications.

The date of enactment, commencement, notification of rules and application of a requirement to a specific establishment are not interchangeable. A compliance decision requires the relevant provision, notification, appropriate Government, rules and facts.

Central Rules, 2026

The Ministry’s Labour Codes repository lists the four Central Rules, 2026, notified on 8 May 2026, along with the Acts, commencement/implementation documents, further notifications and FAQs. Central Rules are relevant to matters where the Central Government is the appropriate Government; they should not automatically be treated as the rules for every State-sphere establishment.

Employer’s initial applicability review

1. Establishment profile

  • Legal entity, business activity and industry.
  • Every workplace, branch, site and State/UT location.
  • Employee and worker categories, headcount and wage data.
  • Contractors, fixed-term arrangements and other workforce categories.

2. Legal applicability

  • Determine the appropriate Government for each issue.
  • Identify statutory definitions, coverage thresholds and exclusions.
  • Check commencement notifications, amendments and exemptions.
  • Identify the applicable Central or State rules and schemes.

3. Internal processes

  • Payroll and wage-component controls.
  • HR policies, standing orders and employee documentation.
  • Working time, safety, health and welfare arrangements.
  • Contractor management and social-security administration.

4. Evidence and ownership

  • Registers, notices, wage slips and payment records.
  • Returns, portal acknowledgements and statutory approvals.
  • Source file, Gazette number, effective date and jurisdiction.
  • Named owner, due date, review frequency and escalation process.
