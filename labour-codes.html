India’s Four Labour Codes

A structured starting point for employers, HR teams and compliance professionals to understand the consolidated labour-law framework.

Content status: Introductory guide | Reviewed: 17 November 2026 | Scope: India

Implementation: the essential distinction

Central Government commencement

The four Labour Codes came into effect from 21 November 2025. The Ministry of Labour & Employment has also published Central Rules and related guidance. Employers must identify the appropriate government, applicable rules, notifications, schemes and establishment-specific requirements before applying a particular provision.

Do not treat “Code enacted”, “Code commenced”, “rules notified” and “a particular compliance obligation applies to this establishment” as interchangeable statements. State and Union Territory rules and the identity of the appropriate government can affect the operational compliance position.

This page is a general educational overview, not a legal opinion or a substitute for checking the latest Gazette notifications and applicable rules.

The four Codes at a glance

CODE 01 · WAGES

Code on Wages, 2019

Consolidates laws relating to wages, minimum wages, payment of wages, bonus and equal remuneration.

Employer compliance themes

  • Determine applicable minimum-wage rates and categories.
  • Review wage periods, payment timelines and authorised deductions.
  • Assess the statutory definition of wages for relevant calculations.
  • Review bonus eligibility, records and applicable returns.
  • Maintain wage, attendance and other prescribed records.

Primary statute: Act 29 of 2019.

CODE 02 · INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS

Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Consolidates provisions relating to trade unions, standing orders and industrial disputes, including specified provisions concerning lay-off, retrenchment and closure.

Employer compliance themes

  • Review trade union recognition and registration obligations.
  • Assess standing-order applicability and certification requirements.
  • Review grievance-redressal and industrial-dispute processes.
  • Check notice, permission and compensation requirements before workforce actions.
  • Maintain prescribed registers, notices and records.

Primary statute: Act 35 of 2020.

CODE 03 · SOCIAL SECURITY

Code on Social Security, 2020

Consolidates laws concerning provident fund, employees’ state insurance, gratuity, maternity benefit, employees’ compensation and social security for specified categories including unorganised, gig and platform workers.

Employer compliance themes

  • Determine establishment and employee coverage under relevant chapters and schemes.
  • Review registration, contributions, returns and payment timelines.
  • Assess gratuity, maternity benefit and employees’ compensation obligations.
  • Maintain employee, wage, contribution and benefit records.
  • Check current schemes, rules and notified thresholds before calculation.

Primary statute: Act 36 of 2020.

CODE 04 · OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY

OSH & Working Conditions Code, 2020

Consolidates provisions on occupational safety, health and working conditions for covered establishments and categories of workers, including provisions concerning contract labour and inter-State migrant workers.

Employer compliance themes

  • Determine establishment coverage, registration and licensing requirements.
  • Assess health, safety, welfare and working-condition standards.
  • Review working hours, leave, overtime and appointment-letter requirements.
  • Check contractor and contract-labour responsibilities where applicable.
  • Maintain prescribed registers, notices, returns and safety records.

Primary statute: Act 37 of 2020.

Employer’s first-step compliance review

Use this as an initial scoping list. It is not a complete statutory audit checklist.

Review area Questions to establish Evidence to collect
Establishment profile What is the nature of business, location, headcount and type of establishment? Registration certificates, licences, organisation chart and employee strength.
Appropriate government Is the Central or State Government the appropriate government for the relevant provision? Establishment details and applicable notifications.
Coverage and thresholds Which Code chapters, schemes and thresholds apply? Headcount, wage data, contractor data and employee records.
Rules and notifications Which Central or State rules, schemes and exemptions govern the obligation? Latest Gazette notifications and official department circulars.
Existing processes Do payroll, HR, safety and industrial-relations procedures reflect current requirements? Payroll registers, policies, standing orders and safety records.
Ongoing compliance Who owns each filing, payment, display, register and renewal? Responsibility matrix, compliance calendar and submission/payment proof.
