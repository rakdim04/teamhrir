ABOUT TEAMHRIR

Independent Knowledge. Practical Application.

TeamHrIr is an independent digital knowledge platform focused on Indian Labour Codes, EPFO and ESIC.

Our objective is to simplify regulatory information through practical guides, employer checklists, digital resources and source-linked updates.

Our materials are educational and informational and do not replace advice based on the facts of a particular establishment or case.
